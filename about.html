Artist biography

Shengpeng Song

Artist
Based in Germany
Painting

Shengpeng Song is a Chinese artist based in Germany working primarily in painting. His background includes studies in Media and Animation at the China Academy of Art, an MA in Oil Painting at the China Academy of Oil Painting in Beijing, and postgraduate study in Creative Arts at the University for the Creative Arts in the United Kingdom.

His work has been presented in exhibitions and artist residencies in China, the United Kingdom and Germany, including a solo exhibition in Margate and residencies at the University for the Creative Arts and Alte Schule Hohenstein.

While his earlier work focused strongly on faces and the human figure, his recent practice moves toward open and narrative forms. Rather than depicting a fixed subject, he is interested in what a figure can carry: memory, vulnerability, uncertainty, desire, conflict and moments of inner transformation.

Artist Statement

Making space for inward attention.

In an era overburdened by hyper-information and collective anxiety, my practice is dedicated to building “subtractive sanctuaries” for the human spirit through a refined visual economy.

Deeply informed by classical painting traditions and the Zen philosophy of inward realization, my work strips away explicit temporal markers, social labels and narrative clutter. Focusing on minimalist portraiture and subtle, textured light, I capture closed-eyed figures in states of stillness. These forms are not an escape from reality, but a quiet, introspective gaze that restores a sense of inner order.

I bridge classical oil painting techniques with contemporary textural exploration. I am fascinated by the traces left by sweeping brushstrokes, transitions between low-saturation tonal blocks and the way oblique light animates the canvas surface. Minimal from a distance yet deep upon closer inspection, a painting becomes a spatial container where viewers can project their emotions and find psychological reprieve.

Rather than offering explicit moral instruction or rigid narrative, my work functions as a quiet vessel for human emotion. As external noise fades, leaving light, texture and breath, I aim to provide a contemplative space where the restless mind can settle into clarity and peace.
